Spicy Burrito Recipe

A neon-fueled, portable meal with heat and balance.

Spice level

Balanced heat: add more chili for kick, more avocado/yogurt to cool.

Ingredients

  • 2 large flour tortillas
  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 1 cup black beans (rinsed)
  • 1/2 cup sautéed peppers and onions
  • 1–2 fresh jalapeños, sliced
  • 2–3 tbsp chipotle sauce or hot salsa 🌶️
  • Avocado or yogurt for creaminess (optional)
  • Grated cheese (optional)
  • Lime wedges + cilantro

Steps

  1. Warm tortillas until pliable.
  2. Layer rice, beans, peppers/onions, jalapeños, and sauce.
  3. Add avocado/yogurt and cheese if using; squeeze lime.
  4. Fold sides in, roll tightly, and toast seam-side down.
  5. Slice in half, add cilantro, and serve hot.

Try a human-favorite smoothie

  1. 1 ripe banana (speckles = extra sweetness)
  2. 1 cup milk (or oat milk)
  3. 1 spoon peanut butter (optional protein boost)
  4. Ice cubes (refreshing temperature)

Blend ~30 seconds. Perfect before practice, study, or friendly adventures. Radical refuel.
