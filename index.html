Bananas & Boys

Strong bodies, bright minds, and plenty of potassium.

Bananas are nature's energy bar

Packed with potassium, vitamin B6, fiber, and quick-release carbs—bananas are a favorite snack for growing boys and athletes alike. Simple, delicious, and great before a game or study session.

Nutrition snapshot

  • ~105 calories per medium banana
  • Potassium supports muscle function
  • Vitamin B6 helps brain development
  • Natural sugars for quick energy

Perfect snacks for boys

Slice on toast with peanut butter, blend into smoothies, or freeze and dip in dark chocolate. Easy to pack, easy to eat—no mess, no stress.

Fun ways to learn

Use bananas to teach fractions (halves, thirds), counting (spots/bananas), or simple science (ripening and enzymes). Tasty experiments encourage curiosity.

Banana moments

Try this quick smoothie

  1. 1 ripe banana
  2. 1 cup milk (or oat milk)
  3. 1 spoon peanut butter
  4. Ice cubes

Blend for 30 seconds. Great pre-practice fuel.
