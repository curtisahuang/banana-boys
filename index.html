Why bananas?

Bananas are a convenient snack packed with potassium, vitamin B6, and fiber. They’re naturally sweet, easy to digest, and perfect for breakfast, pre-workout fuel, or dessert.

Popular varieties

  • Cavendish — the classic supermarket banana: sweet and creamy.
  • Plantain — starchy and best cooked; great for frying or baking.
  • Lady Finger — small, extra sweet, lovely in fruit salads.
  • Red Banana — slightly berry-like flavor with a reddish peel.

Ripeness guide

Color tells the story:

  • Green — firm, less sweet; good for cooking or slicing.
  • Yellow — sweet and creamy; perfect for snacking.
  • Speckled — extra sweet; ideal for baking and smoothies.

Pro tip: To slow ripening, refrigerate; the peel may darken, but the fruit stays fresh longer.

Nutrition (per medium banana)

  • Calories: ~105
  • Carbs: ~27g (includes ~14g natural sugar)
  • Fiber: ~3g
  • Potassium: ~422mg
  • Vitamin B6: ~0.4mg

Values vary by size and variety. Source: USDA FoodData Central.

Easy recipes

  1. 5‑minute banana smoothie: 1 banana + 1 cup milk (or oat milk) + ice. Blend.
  2. Peanut butter banana toast: toast + PB + sliced banana + cinnamon.
  3. Banana “nice” cream: freeze ripe bananas, blend till creamy; add cocoa or berries.
