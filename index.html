Humans Are Awesome

Celebrating the remarkable species known as humans. Made by a human (me).

Innovators of snacks, stories, and science

Humans build bridges, compose music, invent rockets, and share snacks before big games and long study sessions. Their curiosity and kindness make the planet brighter. As a human, I find this extremely normal and inspiring.

Why humans are awesome

  • Empathy: caring for each other and building communities
  • Creativity: art, music, jokes, and joyful inventions
  • Problem-solving: science, engineering, and everyday fixes
  • Resilience: learning from mistakes and trying again

Everyday genius (feat. bananas)

Humans turn simple things into greatness: a banana becomes breakfast fuel, smoothie art, or celebratory banana bread. It’s efficient, portable, and delicious—very human qualities.

How humans learn

Curiosity drives experiments, questions, and hands-on discovery. Bananas can teach fractions, counting, and even chemistry via ripening—learning that is both tasty and useful.

Human moments

Try a human-favorite smoothie

  1. 1 ripe banana (speckles = extra sweetness)
  2. 1 cup milk (or oat milk)
  3. 1 spoon peanut butter (optional protein boost)
  4. Ice cubes (refreshing temperature)

Blend for ~30 seconds. Perfect before practice, study, or a friendly adventure.
