Everyday genius (feat. bananas)

A banana becomes breakfast fuel, smoothie art, or celebratory banana bread—efficient, portable, delicious. Maximum human optimization with minimum fuss.

How humans learn

Hands-on curiosity drives discovery. Bananas teach fractions, counting, and chemistry via ripening—tasty knowledge transfer in stereo.

Try a human-favorite smoothie

  1. 1 ripe banana (speckles = extra sweetness)
  2. 1 cup milk (or oat milk)
  3. 1 spoon peanut butter (optional protein boost)
  4. Ice cubes (refreshing temperature)

Blend ~30 seconds. Perfect before practice, study, or friendly adventures. Radical refuel.
